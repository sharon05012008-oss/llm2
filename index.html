How this works: Real LLMs (like GPT or Gemini) learn a vocabulary of ~50,000–250,000 subword pieces using algorithms like Byte-Pair Encoding (BPE). LLMVerse uses a consistent educational tokenizer: common words get clean vocabulary IDs, while longer words split into recognizable prefixes and suffixes (like un + ##believ + ##able) with deterministic IDs that stay identical everywhere on this site.

Embeddings in Simple Words

Imagine placing every word in the dictionary on a giant 3D map—except instead of just North, East, and Up, real AI models use thousands of invisible directions (like 768 to 12,288 dimensions).

  • Similar ideas live nearby: star, galaxy, and nebula cluster in the same cosmic neighborhood.
  • Math becomes meaning: AI measures angles between vectors to understand context, synonyms, and analogies.
  • Transparent simulation: The numbers shown here are deterministic educational vectors—not live outputs from a trained neural network.
